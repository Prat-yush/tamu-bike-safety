TAMU Bike Rack Safety

Reported theft history near TAMU College Station bike racks, by area.

How this works

Every two weeks, TAMU UPD publishes public Crime Alert bulletins for stolen electric bikes and scooters (a Clery Act requirement). This site scrapes those alerts, matches each reported location to the nearest defined campus bike-rack area, and grades each area from A+ to F based on how many qualifying reports it has relative to how much rack capacity it hosts.

A grade of A+ with no reports does not mean "verified safe" — it means no theft has been reported there in the data collected so far. Always lock your bike properly regardless of area grade.
