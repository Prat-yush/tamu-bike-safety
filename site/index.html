TAMU Bike Rack Safety

Reported theft history near TAMU College Station bike racks, by area.

    Based on historical reports only — not a guarantee.

    How this works

    Every two weeks, TAMU UPD publishes public Crime Alert bulletins for stolen electric bikes and scooters (a Clery Act requirement). This site scrapes those alerts, matches each reported location to the nearest defined campus bike-rack area, and scores it one of two ways. Areas with at least one qualifying report get a letter grade, A+ to F, weighing recent reports more than older ones and blending each area's own history with the campus-wide average based on how much rack capacity (i.e. how much evidence) that area has — a handful of racks with one old report isn't graded as confidently as a large rack area with a real track record. Areas with zero reports get no letter grade at all — just a neutral "No Data" label, since a clean history and an under-reported one look identical from this data.

    "No Data" does not mean "verified safe" — it means no theft has been reported there in the data collected so far, which could mean it's genuinely low-risk or just that nothing has been reported yet. Always lock your bike properly regardless of area grade.
